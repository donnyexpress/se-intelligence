# Using AI tools for SE, from Requirements Generation and Management to Risk Identification, Analysis, and Management

## TL;DR

Our research aims to demonstrate the efficacy of LLMs in interacting with Systems Modeling Language (SysML) models. This includes not just meeting predefined requirements but also uncovering derived requirements essential for mitigating risks. We propose a detailed case study that showcases the integration of LLMs in streamlining the systems engineering process.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Ryan Bell (Naval Postgraduate School) - ryan.bell@nps.edu; Ryan Longshore (Naval Postgraduate School) - ryan.longshore@nps.edu; Ray Madachy (Naval Postgraduate School) - rjmadach@nps.edu.
