# Systems Engineering Innovation through ‘Futures’ Methods

## TL;DR

This presentation will discuss how concepts and methods from the emerging field of futures studies can be applied to systems engineering to promote more far-reaching and innovative technology solutions.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Bonnie Johnson (Naval Postgraduate School) - bwjohnson@nps.edu.
