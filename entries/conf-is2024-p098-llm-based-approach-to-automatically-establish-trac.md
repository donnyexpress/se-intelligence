# LLM-based Approach to Automatically Establish Traceability between Requirements and MBSE

## TL;DR

Automatically establishing traceability between requirements and Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) is crucial for ensuring safety standards in development. Our session introduces a tool that utilizes Large Language Models (LLMs) to establish links between requirements and MBSE semi-automatically. We present evaluation results comparing different approaches, assessing recall, precision, and F_2 score.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Maria Bonner (Project Lead) - maria.bonner@siemens.com; Marc Zeller (Safety Expert) - marc.zeller@siemens.com; Gabor Schulz (Software Engineer) - gabor.schulz@siemens.com; Ana Savu (Software Engineer) - ana.savu@siemens.com.
