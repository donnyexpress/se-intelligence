# Exploration of SysML V2 Capabilities to bridge the gap from System Modeling to Network Design

## TL;DR

The promise of SysML V2 is on the verge becoming reality. The new SysML V2 standard is being released in 2024 bringing a new world of interoperability to System Engineers.This presentation will introduce you to that new world with a example familiar to many industries.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Tony Komar (Siemens) - tony.komar@siemens.com.
