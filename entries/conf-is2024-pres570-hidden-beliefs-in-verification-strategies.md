# Hidden Beliefs in Verification Strategies

## TL;DR

The objective of this research is to study the formation of engineers’ beliefs during the engineering and design of systems. The hypothesis is that engineers possess hidden belief networks that are used to assess the correct operation of a system, and will make those only explicit if certain design features conflict with those structures.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Joanna Joseph (University of Arizona) - joannajoseph@arizona.edu; Alejandro Salado (University of Arizona) - alejandrosalado@arizona.edu.
