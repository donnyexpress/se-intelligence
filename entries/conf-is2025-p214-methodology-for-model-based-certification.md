# Methodology for Model-Based Certification

## TL;DR

This paper presents a methodology for Model-Based Certification (MBC). As Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) gains acceptance in industry, efficiencies can be realized by including more aspects of the systems engineering Vee model within the MBSE system model. This paper proposes a process for Model-Based Certification, wherein all aspects of a system of interest and the data needed for certification can be contained within a Systems Modeling Language (SysML) based model.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 35th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2025) in Ottawa, Canada, July 26–31, 2025. Authors: Jay Silverman (Old Dominion University) - jsilv019@odu.edu; Holly Handley (Old Dominion University) - hhandley@odu.edu.
