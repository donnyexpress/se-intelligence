# Enhancing Industry 4.0 Transformation Success with a Solution Debt Playbook

## TL;DR

This presentation provides a solution debt playbook with actionable guidance to help programs or Industry 4.0 initiatives systematically avoid, manage, and retire debt for tighter integration and enhanced execution.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Heidi Davidz (ManTech International) - Heidi.Davidz@mantech.com; Matthew Taylor (MxD) - Matt.Taylor@mxdusa.org; Mark Schriner (ManTech International) - Mark.Schriner@mantech.com.
