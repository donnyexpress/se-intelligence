# Early Validation of SysML Architectures by Extending MBSE with Co-Simulation using FMI and SSP

## TL;DR

In this paper we discuss the current status of Co-simulation in MBSE and how we might extend the current capabilities using the SSP standard.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Johan Cederbladh (Mälardalen university) - johan.cederbladh@mdu.se; Daniel Krems (AVL List) - daniel.krems@avl.com.
