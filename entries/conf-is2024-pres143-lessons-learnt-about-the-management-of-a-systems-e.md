# Lessons learnt about the management of a Systems Engineering trainings framework: A Safran Group return on experience

## TL;DR

Past, present and future of the MBSE training framework for the Safran group. Feedbacks of the transformation journey of the group.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Nicolas Gueit (Safran Landing Systems) - nicolas.gueit@safrangroup.com; Julien Castex (Safran SA) - julien.castex@safrangroup.com; Julien Maille (Safran Aircraft Engines) - julien.maille@safrangroup.com; Stephen Perun (Safran Aerosystems) - stephen.perun@safrangroup.com.
