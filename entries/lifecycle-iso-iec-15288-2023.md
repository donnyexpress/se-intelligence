# ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 — what changed in the revision

## TL;DR
The 2023 revision of 15288 reorganized the technical management processes (now Part 2) and added explicit treatment of human factors, system security, and AI/ML-enabled systems. It remains a process reference, not a methodology.

## Why it matters
If you work on systems that touch government, defense, or regulated industries, 15288 is the standard that buyers and procurement reference. Knowing what's in the 2023 edition — and what your contracts still demand from the 2015 version — is table stakes for SE leads.

## Key ideas
- 30+ processes grouped into 4 families: Technical, Technical Management, Agreement, Organizational Project-Enabling.
- 2023 revision: stronger alignment with ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 (architecture description) and ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 (requirements).
- AI/ML systems now have explicit hooks in the verification process.
- Human factors is no longer a "specialty" — it has first-class process entries.
- The standard is intentionally methodology-neutral (V-model, agile, waterfall, hybrid all fit).

## My take
Don't try to implement 15288 top-down. Map your existing process to it, find the gaps your customers care about, and close those first. The standard is a vocabulary, not a workflow. If a process doesn't help you ship a better system, the audit will pass anyway.
