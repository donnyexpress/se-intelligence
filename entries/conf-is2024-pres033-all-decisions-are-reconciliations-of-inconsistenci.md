# All Decisions Are Reconciliations of Inconsistencies: Preparing for the Digital Thread and Machine Learning

## TL;DR

This presentation characterizes, in an unconventional way, the diverse decisions that occur across system life cycles. The benefits of this framing are that it (1) unifies understanding of the life cycle, (2) enables the organization to gain from the digital thread, and (3) prepares for machine learning.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: William Schindel (ICTT System Sciences) - schindel@ictt.com.
