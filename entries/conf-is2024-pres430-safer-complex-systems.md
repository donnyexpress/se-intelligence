# Safer Complex Systems

## TL;DR

To enable safer complex systems, we need effective governance with mechanisms for planned adaptation to ensure appropriate agility and resilience. This presentation will provide an overview of recent work by INCOSE and the Engineering X: Safer Complex Systems Initiative.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Meaghan O'Neil (System Design and Strategy Ltd) - meaghan.oneil@gmail.com; Duncan Kemp (Ministry of Defence) - duncan@17media.co.uk.
