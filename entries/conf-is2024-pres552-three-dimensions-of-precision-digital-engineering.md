# Three Dimensions of Precision Digital Engineering

## TL;DR

While the digital representation for digital twins, digital threads, MBSE, and digital simulations is the central concern for a Digital Engineering approach, there are two additional engineering concerns that must be addressed for a successful DE solution. We refer to these three concerns as the three dimensions of Precision Digital Engineering: Multi-discipline, Multi-product, and Multi-baseline. Carefully separating each of these concerns enables a precise holistic solution for advanced DE.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Charles Krueger (BigLever) - ckrueger@biglever.com.
