# An holistic view of the implementation of the Open Architecture Approach in military systems

## TL;DR

Open System Architectures offer benefits in terms of cost and time in the delivery and support of military systems, but the accomplishment in its implementation has varied over time, governments, and is viewed differently across the supply chain. This presentation will address the different perceptions on the open architecture approach, and the challenges for customers and suppliers that must be addressed to enable the intended benefits in life-cycle management of military systems.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Raquel Lampaca Vieira Radoman (Loughborough University) - r.lampaca-vieira-radoman@lboro.ac.uk; Michael Henshaw (Loughborough University) - m.j.d.henshaw@lboro.ac.uk; Melanie King (Loughborough University) - M.R.N.King@lboro.ac.uk; Tim Rabbets (QinetiQ ltd) - tjrabbets@qinetiq.com.
