# Survey of LLM Applications for Systems Engineering

## TL;DR

Much of systems engineering work is carried out through the use of text, in the form of requirements, specifications, and other documents. Generative AI, especially Large language models are a natural fit to support many aspects of systems engineering. In this presentation, we survey how large language models (LLMs) are being used in systems engineering applications. Live demonstrations of many of the techniques will be included.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Barclay Brown (Collins Aerospace) - barclay@barclaybrown.com.
