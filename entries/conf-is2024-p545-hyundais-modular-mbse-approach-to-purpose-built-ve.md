# Hyundai’s Modular MBSE Approach to ‘Purpose Built Vehicle’ Architecture Development

## TL;DR

This presentation describes the modular MBSE approach applied to vehicle performance development at Hyundai. Hyundai Motor Group aspires to transition to a smart mobility paradigm where the boundaries between systems blur. The paper describes a MBSE approach applied in the context of smart mobility system of systems that can be incorporated to efficiently develop modular systems together with the automotive supply chain.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Ilsoo Jeong (Hyundai Motor Group) - isjeong@hyundai.com; Shashank Alai (Siemens DISW) - shashank.alai@siemens.com; Jaekap Joo (Hyundai Motor Group) - jkjoo@hyundai.com; Michael Baloh (Siemens DISW) - michael.baloh@siemens.com; Sunkil Yun (Hyundai Motor Group) - piesun@hyundai.com; +2 more.
