# A Comprehensive Risk Assessment Methodology for Extended Product Lifecycles

## TL;DR

The content of this presentation is based upon on-going internal research and development by Georgia Tech Research Institute. The authors believe that the concept and mechanics for this methodology are mature enough for discussion however they have not been applied to real world applications as of this writing.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Jason Stroup (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - jason.stroup@gtri.gatech.edu; Alton Schultheis (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - alton.schultheis@gtri.gatech.edu; Jeremy Doerr (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - Jeremy.Doerr@gtri.gatech.edu; Iqbal Ahmed (Georgia Tech Research Institute (GTRI)) - iqbal.ahmed@gtri.gatech.edu.
