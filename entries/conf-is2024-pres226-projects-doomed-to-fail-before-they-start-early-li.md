# Projects Doomed to fail before they start - Early Lifecycle Activities are missing

## TL;DR

Infrastructure Projects are rarely completed on time or within budget. This has become a "norm" that is begrudgingly tolerated by a jaded public. The societal waste is very high, on aggregate, in many of the highly developed nations on earth. The Infrastructure domain represents a multi-trillion-dollar global opportunity to improve deployment of systems engineering. This presentation will provide evidence to support the failure assertions, analyze root causes and present pragmatic solutions

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Dale Brown (Hatch) - dale.brown@hatch.com; Jonnathan Sprakes (Stantec) - Jonathan.Sprakes@stantec.com.
