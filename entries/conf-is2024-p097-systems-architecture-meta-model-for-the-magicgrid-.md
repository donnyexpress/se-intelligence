# Systems Architecture Meta-Model for the MagicGrid Framework

## TL;DR

This paper studies SysML as the standard language to model systems, and MagicGrid as the framework to bridge the gap between Systems Engineering (SE) terminology described in the INCOSE Systems Engineering Handbook and SysML specification. It proposes a new systems engineering meta-model to describe a common SE terminology and bind it to the SysML concepts.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Aiste Aleksandraviciene (Dassault Systemes) - aiste.aleksandraviciene@3ds.com; Zilvinas Strolia (Dassault Systemes) - zilvinas.strolia@3ds.com; Aurelijus Morkevicius (Department of Information Systems Kaunas University of Technology and Dassault; Systemes) - aurelijus.morkevicius@3ds.com.
