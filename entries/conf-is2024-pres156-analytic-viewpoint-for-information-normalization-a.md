# Analytic Viewpoint for Information Normalization (AVIaN): A model-based analytic viewpoint to promote consistency in systems engineering practice

## TL;DR

AVIaN or Analytic Viewpoint for Information Normalization is a model-based analytic framework that frames stakeholder concerns related to the analysis and assessment of an architecture against an engineering domain, such as MOSA or Cyber.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Richard Wise (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - richard.wise@gtri.gatech.edu.
