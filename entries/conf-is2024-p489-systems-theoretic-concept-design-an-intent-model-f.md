# Systems-Theoretic Concept Design: An Intent Model for Early Concept Generation

## TL;DR

Early design concept generation for major defense systems often focus no advanced technologies & future capabilities when a more top-down approach might be more applicable. Systems-Theoretic Concept Design is a new framework to generate early concepts for new systems that better captures intent using a novel application of systems theory.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Alexander Hillman (Massachusetts Institute of Technology / US Air Force) - ahillman@mit.edu; Nancy Leveson (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) - leveson@mit.edu; William Young (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) - wyoung@mit.edu.
