# Integrating IV&V into a generative AI Enterprise work culture

## TL;DR

Because of the power of LLM’s to generate code & narrative text, it has never been more important to apply strong IV&V principles. to ensure that the results generated by GPT models are accurate, reliable, & meet the required standard. Enterprises must prioritize IV&V in the development & deployment of GPT models. This can involve rigorous testing and validation, transparent and ethical use of data, and involving users in the design & development process.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Jason Blevins (National Railroad Passenger Corporation ( Amtrak)) - blevinj@amtrak.com.
