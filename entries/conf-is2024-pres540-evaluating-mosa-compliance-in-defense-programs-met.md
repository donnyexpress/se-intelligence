# Evaluating MOSA Compliance in Defense Programs: Methodologies and Practical Approaches

## TL;DR

Session participants attending the "Evaluating MOSA Compliance in Defense Programs: Methodologies and Practical Approaches" technical presentation will gain a deeper understanding of MOSA compliance requirements and methodologies. Participants can also apply this knowledge practically in their respective roles.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Nadine Geier (Office of the Undersecretary of Defense (OUSD(R&E)) Systems Engineering) Nadine.M.Geier.Civ@mail.mil; John Tindle (SAIC) - John.R.Tindle.Ctr@mail.mil; Monique Ofori (SAIC) - monique.pkservices@gmail.com.
