# Developing a Model-Based Systems Engineering Tool for Cybersecurity Risk Management of Micro-Electronic Devices

## TL;DR

This presentation outlines a method to quantify cyber threats associated with micro-electronics. The method utilizes MBSE as a tool to implement a cyber-threat assessment model. The model integrates a mathematical quantification of these threats to produce a visualization of the results in a 5x5 risk matrix. This tool will help users identify unique threat vectors and analyze counter-measure strategies to mitigate the effects on system performance, safety, and security.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024.
