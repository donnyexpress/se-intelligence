# An MBSE group project challenge as a learning experience for Masters degree students

## TL;DR

This presentation reports on setting up a group project challenge for Masters students at Loughborough University, 15 groups over three years. The challenge set was a genuine leading-edge problem facing industry – how to keep multiple models of a system under development in step through joint configuration control of design changes in each model. The quality and variety of results, which will be demonstrated, have been very good, and have proved an excellent discriminator of student capability.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Paul Davies (thesystemsengineer.uk) - paul@thesystemsengineer.uk.
