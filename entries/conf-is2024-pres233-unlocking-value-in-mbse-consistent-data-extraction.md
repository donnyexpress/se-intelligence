# Unlocking Value in MBSE – Consistent Data Extraction & Visualisation from SysML Models with Rule-based Analysis

## TL;DR

Unlock the full potential of Model Based Systems Engineering! Join us in exploring our solution in addressing the challenges in extracting data from SysML models and tracking project progress. The tool agnostic queries applied to the underlying models generate insightful metrics that can be visualised on open-source dashboard platforms such as Grafana. Experience a live demonstration, empowering engineers and stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Liam Biddle (L3Harris) - l.biddle96@gmail.com; Peri Mehmet (The Systems Thinking & Eng. Co Ltd) - peri.mehmet@systems-tec.co.uk.
