# The A to Z for Implementing a Digital Transformation on a Systems Project

## TL;DR

Digital Transformations (DT) continue to change our products, systems, services, and the way we work. We are moving to model-based approaches faster with knowledge sharing exponentially increasing. Will we be ready for this future? To tackle such challenges we will present the “A to Z guide” for a DT, including the digitalization of applicable SE practices. This guide is built from experience on multiple projects, across different organizations and countries, and is applicable to all domains.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Kerry Lunney (Thales) - kjlunney@tpg.com.au; Stephane Bonnet (Thales) - stephane.bonnet@thalesgroup.com.
