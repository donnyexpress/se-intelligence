# Extending SysML Model Federation to Support Systems of Systems Multilevel Security Development

## TL;DR

This paper builds on previously published work on SysML model federation in support of SoS architecting, extending that methodology to address the unique challenges of development in the presence of MLS policies. The paper describes a federation approach spanning classified networks, method to allow an individual system model to be divided across classification levels, and a relationship based classification profile openly published for use.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Chris Swickline (SAIC) - crs098@gmail.com.
