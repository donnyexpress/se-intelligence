# The Nature of Technical Debt in the Development of Descriptive Models in MBSE

## TL;DR

This paper describes how the technical debt concept widely used in the software domain—-rework deferred to the future for expediency-—needs to be modified to the domain of descriptive models. To illustrate the model technical debt concept, several examples of modeling principles pertaining to model purpose and implementation are described along with their implications on model technical debt.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Ryan Noguchi (The Aerospace Corporation) - ryan.a.noguchi@aero.org.
