# Mission Possible: Deploying MBSE Model Libraries for Optimal Systems Development

## TL;DR

Embark on deploying an MBSE model library for diverse industries. Explore tailored team training, define library scope, and establish governance. Witness seamless integration for optimal support in developing complex systems. This adventure promises success through careful planning and execution, focusing on scope, training, collaborative governance, and streamlined integration. Join us to enhance efficiency in the world of MBSE.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Andrew Gabel (The Boeing Company) - andrew.j.gabel@boeing.com; Ariel Mordoch (The Boeing Company) - ariel.mordoch@boeing.com.
