# How to Develop a Digitization Plan for Standards

## TL;DR

As companies embark on digital initiatives, they are finding that they use large libraries of standards that consolidate requirements and information from a variety of sources. These document-based standards are distributed via PDF, making them difficult to integrate into digital product design and system modeling tools. Attend this session to learn how to plan out digitization of your company's standards.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Leslie McKay (SAE International) - leslie.mckay@sae.org.
