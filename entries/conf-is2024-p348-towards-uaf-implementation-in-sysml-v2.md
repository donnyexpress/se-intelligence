# Towards UAF Implementation in SysML V2

## TL;DR

With the major update of SysML, all related standards need to be redesigned. The Unified Architecture Framework (UAF) is not an exception. Currently, the development of UAF based on SysML V2 is in the very early stages, and various organizations involved in its development are independently researching the way forward. This paper describes one of the research projects to test the feasibility of SysML V2 to address UAF community needs.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024.
