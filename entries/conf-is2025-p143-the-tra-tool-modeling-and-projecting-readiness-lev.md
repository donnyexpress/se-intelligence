# The TRA Tool: Modeling and Projecting Readiness Levels with MBSE

## TL;DR

In this document, we discuss the state of the art for technology readiness assessments through the use of established metrics, we propose a Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) approach that streamlines the process, and we provide a specific implementation of this approach with our TRA Tool. The TRA Tool provides a methodology for completing a Technology and System Readiness Assessment in a model-based environment. The tool allows for the efficient collection and management of data on the maturation schedule of each technology and integration of a complex system design in a SysML based architecture model. It produces numerous useful diagram views and metrics to support analysis of a design’s maturity.
