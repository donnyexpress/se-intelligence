# Sustainably Designing Products / Designing Sustainable Products

## TL;DR

Sustainability is an outcome of two interrelated product engineering dimensions.Sustainably Designing Products - becoming more efficient in the actual development/building/creation of products.Designing Sustainable Products - Building a product that meets our efficiency goals to consume less energy, provide new efficiency methods, reusable products, etc

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Jim Marsh (INCOSE Member) - jim.marsh@us.ibm.com; Gianluca Monticone (IBM) - gianluca.monticone@it.ibm.com; Edmund Mayer (IBM) - emayer@us.ibm.com.
