# Easing SE implementation in daily life

## TL;DR

This presentation is a view of an instruction layer for SE, which allows organizations to implement and ramp up quickly the relevant processes in the design phase of a system. To onboard new beginners at SE, one must lower the barrier to ease the use of SE. Or as we say in our company: "What exactly are organizations new to SE supposed to do next Monday 8am with SE and how?" This presentation gives you practical takeaways for this question.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Henrik Balslev (CEO - ESEP) - hb@syseng.dk.
