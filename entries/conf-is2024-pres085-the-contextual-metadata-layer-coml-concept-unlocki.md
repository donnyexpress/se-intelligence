# The Contextual Metadata Layer (CoML) concept - unlocking collaboration in an uncertain/ BANI world

## TL;DR

The Contextual Metadata Layer, in conjunction with the concepts, processes and methods proposed in the INCOSE SE Handbook provide the needed clarity on how we may realize complex products through effective collaboration in an uncertain BANI world.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Kyle Hall (Airbus) - kyle.hall@airbus.com; Etienne Coetzee (Airbus) - etienne.coetzee@airbus.com; Alan Lang (Airbus) - allan.lang@airbus.com.
