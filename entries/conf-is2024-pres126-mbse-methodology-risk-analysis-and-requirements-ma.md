# MBSE Methodology: Risk Analysis and Requirements Management

## TL;DR

Verifying a system of interest functionality through a formal engineering qualification or certification process is an essential step that proves that a system complies with its requirements. In this actively developing methodology for MBSE-assisted requirements verification, a fully traceable set of hierarchical system requirements integrated with the system architecture is used to qualify a system.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Nicole Davis (Los Alamos National Laboratory) - nmdavis@lanl.gov; Kaelin Glover (Los Alamos National Laboratory) - kglover@lanl.gov; Katelyn Yeamans (Los Alamos National Laboratory) - katelyn@lanl.gov.
