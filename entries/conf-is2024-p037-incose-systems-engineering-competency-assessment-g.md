# INCOSE Systems Engineering Competency Assessment Guide Systems Modeling Language (SysML) Model Description

## TL;DR

The INCOSE Systems Engineering Competency Framework and INCOSE Systems Engineering Competency Assessment Guide provide a requirement definition for 37 systems engineering competencies across 5 different proficiency levels and sample evidence against each of these indicator requirements. This paper describes the development of a Systems Modeling Language (SysML) model that provides a digital representation of the architecture framework and associated requirement and evidence entry databases.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Juan Amenabar (Leidos) - juan.p.amenabar@leidos.com; Clifford Whitcomb (Cornell University) - caw356@cornell.edu.
