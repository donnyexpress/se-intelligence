# MBPLE Adoption in the European Aviation, Defense and Automotive Industries

## TL;DR

This paper focuses on the adoption of Model-Based Product Line Engineering (MBPLE) across three European industries, showcasing its potential to address system’s complexity and variability. MBPLE, as combination of feature based PLE and Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE), leverages standards like ISO/IEC 26580 to enable machine-readable models for managing variants and supporting the digital thread across multiple domain-specific assets. The work highlights a common pattern in MBPLE adoption, from initial motivation to practical implementation, in terms of process, methods, information model, toolchain and organization, while noting differences driven by industry-specific contexts.
