# Exploring the Executable SysML Capabilities to Integrate and Operate Hardware in the Loop

## TL;DR

Effective modeling and integration are critical SE capabilities. The goal of this paper is to explore the capabilities of SysML to model and control HIL. The SysML model of a weapon system was created. With help of standards (SCXML, fUML), SysML can be made executable and used for simulation purposes. While SysML was selected to provide a digital model of the weapon system, a LEGO Mindstorms EV3 development kit was selected to create a mockup of a physical system.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Alexander Yeiser (Naval Postgraduate School) - alexander.yeiser@nps.edu; Saulius Pavalkis (Dassault Systemes) - Saulius.PAVALKIS@3ds.com; Oleg Yakimenko (Naval Postgraduate School) - oayakime@nps.edu.
