# A Case Study of AI Usage within the INCOSE Technical Process

## TL;DR

The purpose of the research described herein is to analyze the use of AI platforms by a user implementing a generic Product Development Process (PDP) mapped onto the INCOSE Technical Process (ITP) and to identify the advantages and disadvantages.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Pete Chagnon (University of Detroit Mercy) - chagnopa@udmercy.edu; Rachelle Forney (University of Detroit Mercy) - forneyra@udmercy.edu; Kenneth Harkenrider (University of Detroit Mercy) - harkenke@udmercy.edu; Emily Wood (University of Detroit Mercy) - wooden@udmercy.edu; Yiyang Zhang (University of Detroit Mercy) - zhangyi13@udmercy.edu; +1 more.
