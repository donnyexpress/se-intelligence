# Tradestudy for Platforming strategy and Product Line Engineering

## TL;DR

Is managing variants of huge and complex systems on your mind? You have a digital solution to manage and deploy PLE, but not sure where to begin? Or are you simply looking to understand how to come up with platforming strategy in the first place? If any of your answers is yes, this presentation will give you insights on how the authors have strategized, developed and managed complex Product Portfolios, by identifying the right set of variants.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Mudit Mittal (BlueKei Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) - mudit@blue-kei.com; Stueti Gupta (BlueKei Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) - stueti@blue-kei.com.
