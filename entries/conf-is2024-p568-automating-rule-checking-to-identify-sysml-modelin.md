# Automating Rule-Checking to Identify SysML Modeling Errors: A Preliminary Study in a Classroom Environment

## TL;DR

This paper provides an empirical insight into this benefit through a study of models developed by students in a graduate MBSE course. The study shows that the term project models resulted in nearly zero latent errors when non-stylistic rules are concerned, with most of the latent errors categorized stylistic rather than fundamental violations.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Michael Vinarcik (College of Engineering and Science, University of Detroit Mercy) - vinarcmi@udmercy.edu; Sukhwan Jung (Department of Systems and Industrial Engineering, University of Arizona) shjung@arizona.edu; Alajandro Salado (Department of Systems and Industrial Engineering, University of Arizona) alejandrosalado@arizona.edu.
