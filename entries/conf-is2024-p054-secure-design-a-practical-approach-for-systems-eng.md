# Secure Design: A Practical Approach for Systems Engineers

## TL;DR

Systems Engineering Vision 2035 states that security will be as foundational a perspective in systems design as system performance and safety. This paper informs such a perspective, borrowing heavily from concepts of inherently safe. Inherently secure design is a design where hazards, susceptibilities, and vulnerabilities are eliminated to the extent possible and the remaining ones are controlled, while still enabling the system to meet performance requirements.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Mark Winstead (MITRE) - mwinstead@mitre.org.
