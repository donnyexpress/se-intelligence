# Taming the beast: Best Practices of Extending SysML V2

## TL;DR

While developing Systems Modeling Language (SysML) version 2 libraries for Unified Architecture Framework (UAF) version 2, we pushed the boundaries of the SysML V2 extension mechanism. This journey involved extensive collaboration with SysML V2 leadership and making tough decisions to move forward. It was a pioneering experience, filled with insights that we’re eager to share with anyone looking to extend SysML V2 in their own environment. From libraries to metadata, text to graphics, and tool enhancements, we’ll cover everything you need to tame the beast.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 35th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2025) in Ottawa, Canada, July 26–31, 2025. Authors: Aurelijus Morkevicius (Department of Information Systems Kaunas University of Technology and Dassault; Systemes) - aurelijus.morkevicius@3ds.com; Gintare Krisciuniene (Department of Information Systems Kaunas University of Technology and Dassault; Systemes) - gintare.krisciuniene@3ds.com.
