# Model-Based Decision Support using Test and Evaluation: A Lightweight Architecture Approach

## TL;DR

A standardized decision support tool to support test prioritization and decision-making is a prerequisite to achieving on-time delivery of weapon systems that are adequately tested and vetted by decision makers within distributed organization such as the defense industrial base. To address this need, a model-based reference architecture for the standardization of the Integrated Decision Support Key (IDSK) data and decision formats is presented in this paper.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Awele Anyanhun (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - awele.anyanhun@gtri.gatech.edu; Craig Arndt (Georgia Tech Research Institute) - craig.arndt@gtri.gatech.edu; Jeremy Werner (United States Department of Defense, Office of the Director, Operational Test and Evaluation); - jeremy.s.werner.civ@mail.mil.
