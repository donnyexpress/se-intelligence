# Context-Based Systems Engineering

## TL;DR

Context-Based Systems Engineering, a new problem/system decomposition approach. Instead of the traditional way, where stakeholder requirements are transformed into system requirements on the system-of-interest, stakeholder requirements are decomposed into requirements for modified context subsystems. This will enable executable requirments and very early integration, verification and validation.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Johan Bredin (Saab AB) - johan.bredin@saabgroup.com.
