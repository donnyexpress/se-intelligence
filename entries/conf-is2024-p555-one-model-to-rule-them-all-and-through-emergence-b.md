# One Model to Rule them All … and Through Emergence, Bind Them

## TL;DR

In a contemporary context of models being central to understanding systems and the model-based engineering of systems, this paper explores the possibility of a meta "model of systems models”, seeking to find, in “Gandalf’s phraseology”, “One model to rule them all, one model to find them, one model to frame them all, and through emergence bind them”.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Jawahar Bhalla (The University of Adelaide) - jb@engineeringsystems.com.au; Stephen Cook (The University of Adelaide) - stephen.cook@adelaide.edu.au; David Harvey (The University of Adelaide) - david.harvey@adelaide.edu.au.
