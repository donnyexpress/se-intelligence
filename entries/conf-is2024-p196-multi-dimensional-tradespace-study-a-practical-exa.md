# Multi-dimensional Tradespace Study – A Practical Example Of Implementation

## TL;DR

Decision making is a critical element of all Systems Engineering activities. It is critical, at least at some core decision points, to not just get compromises between stakeholders, but reliable consensus. Tradespace Study can be one powerful tool for the Systems Engineer to make fact-based decisions while supporting set-based design and agile working methods in parallel.
