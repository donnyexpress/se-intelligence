# AI Starter Kit and Caveats for the Systems Engineer

## TL;DR

AI, and in particular, generative AI, burst abruptly onto the systems engineering scene. The first generative AI made available to the general public just six years ago, yet McKinsey estimates that almost three quarters of all companies use it in at least one of their business units. Engineers are adjusting to this phenomenal rate of change while trying to get their normally fast-paced work done. This paper is intended to provide a brief introduction to the INCOSE member as to how this all happened, how AI can help within the systems engineering life cycle, what cautions and caveats apply, and the results of an experiment with submitting three SE-related questions to 3 popular AI models. The paper results in recommendations for practitioners, managers, and INCOSE regarding AI adoption.

## Why it matters
