# A Classical Modernization of the V-Model

## TL;DR

This paper provides new iconography for the Systems Engineering “V-Model”. The “Arch Model” resets and refreshes the original iterative intent of the “V-Model” in a modern context integrated with digital engineering (DE). We will highlight common misperceptions that reduced the efficacy of the “V-Model” and explore how a “classical Roman engineering” metaphor can inspire a modern view of systems development based on historically successful, foundational engineering.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024.
