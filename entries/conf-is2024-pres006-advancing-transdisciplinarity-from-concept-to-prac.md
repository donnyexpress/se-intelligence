# Advancing Transdisciplinarity from Concept to Practice

## TL;DR

Systems Science uses the concept of transdisciplinarity as a fundamental approach to exploring the nature of systems and better our understanding of how to develop systemic solutions. This presentation will discuss how Systems Science can help Systems Engineers become more transdisciplinary in their practice by focusing on outcomes and how its processes, methods, and tools can support achieving a desired outcome.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Javier Calvo-Amodio (Oregon State University) - Javier.Calvo@oregonstate.edu; James Martin (Aerospace Corporation) - martinqzx@gmail.com.
