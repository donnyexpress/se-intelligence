# A Framework to use Bifurcation Analysis for Insight into Complex Systems Resilience

## TL;DR

The increasing complexity and integration of systems present challenges in understanding and managing these systems, as highlighted by the INCOSE Systems Engineering Vision for 2035. Bifurcation analysis, a mathematical system dynamics technique, offers a different perspective by examining how systems behave under changing conditions. This paper aims to bridge the gap between bifurcation analysis and Resilience Engineering, offering a framework for integrating both approaches.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Rogelio Gracia Otalvaro (Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University) - graciaor@my.erau.edu; Bryan Watson (Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University) - Watsonb3@erau.edu.
