# Implementing MBSE in complex organizations: Moving from Spec-Design-Build to Integrate-THEN-Build practices

## TL;DR

Scaling MBSE requires organization change to take advantage of the many powerful MBSE tools. Given MBSE is not just a tool, its a journey, once organizations know where they are they need to plan a successful MBSE implementation journey. Join us to learn about how to implement MBSE in your organization using standard organization change practices and theory.

## Why it matters

This presentation was delivered at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Mark Sampson (Siemens) - mark.sampson@incose.net; Mark Sampson (Siemens) - mark.e.sampson@outlook.com.
