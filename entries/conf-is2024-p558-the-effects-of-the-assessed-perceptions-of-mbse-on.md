# The Effects of the Assessed Perceptions of MBSE on Adoption

## TL;DR

This session examines the challenge of MBSE adoption through the lens of the diffusion of innovation theory. Data collected through a survey is used to assess how MBSE is perceived by potential adopters and the effect of those perceptions on the adoption rate of MBSE.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 34th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2024) in Dublin, Ireland, July 2–6, 2024. Authors: Daniel Call (Colorado State University) - daniel.r.call@gmail.com; Daniel Herber (Colorado State University) - daniel.herber@colostate.edu; Steven Conrad (Colorado State University) - steve.conrad@colostate.edu.
