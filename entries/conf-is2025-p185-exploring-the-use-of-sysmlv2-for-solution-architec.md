# Exploring the Use of SysMLv2 for Solution Architecture Development with the MagicGrid Framework

## TL;DR

The forthcoming release of SysMLv2 marks a transformative step in systems engineering. This paper examines the application of SysMLv2 concepts within the MagicGrid framework, a widely recognized methodology for model-based systems engineering. The purpose of this study is to explore how SysMLv2 aligns with MagicGrid and identify potential benefits and challenges compared to SysMLv1. The scope includes a comparative case study, focusing on developing solution architecture models using both language versions. This research provides an early evaluation, offering insights to practitioners as they prepare for the anticipated release of SysMLv2 in early 2025.

## Why it matters

This paper was presented at the 35th Annual INCOSE International Symposium (IS 2025) in Ottawa, Canada, July 26–31, 2025. Authors: Aiste Aleksandraviciene (Dassault Systèmes) - aiste.aleksandraviciene@gmail.com; Zilvinas Strolia (Dassault Systèmes) - zilvinas.strolia@3ds.com.
